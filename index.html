Why it doesn't need a babysitter

Every job an attendant does, the booth does on its own.

Staffing is what makes a photobooth expensive. So we took the attendant out of the equation and put their whole job — money, prints, resets, troubleshooting — into the machine.

Tap to pay on the shell, or prepaid by the host.

A card reader is bolted to the booth. Guests tap a card or phone, the session unlocks, the strip prints. Hosts who'd rather nobody reaches for a wallet buy the night outright and the booth runs in unlimited mode instead.

Kiosk mode guests can't break.

It boots straight into the booth app, locks out everything else, times out back to the attract loop after every session, and a watchdog restarts it if anything hangs.

Ribbon and paper counts, jam detection, offline alerts — all pushed to our phones over the booth's own 4G. We can take over the screen remotely mid-party without anyone at the venue lifting a finger.

Prints

Dry, cut and curl-free in twelve seconds.

Dye-sublimation, not inkjet. Strips come out dry to the touch and survive being shoved in a jacket pocket at 1am.

Afterwards

Digital copies before they've left the booth.

Guests scan the on-screen code and their strip lands on their phone — plus animated GIF and boomerang versions. The host gets one gallery link for everything, and a session report the next morning: strips printed, busiest hour, total taken on card.

Start to finish

Four things happen. You're involved in one of them.

  1. 01

    We deliver it

    A wheeled flight case rolls in the service door. Two people, fifteen minutes, one standard power socket. We text you a photo of it set up and lit before we leave.

  2. 02

    Guests run it themselves

    The booth loops an attract screen until someone touches it. Pick a layout, three-two-one, four frames, done. The prompts are big enough to follow at midnight after a few drinks.

  3. 03

    It takes payment and prints

    Card tap or prepaid, then the strip drops into the tray and the digital copy goes to their phone. Between sessions it resets itself and waits for the next group.

  4. 04

    We collect it

    Next morning, on our schedule, not yours. Your gallery link and the night's report arrive by lunchtime.

Straight answers

The things people ask before they book.

Is it genuinely unattended?

Yes. The booth is designed to be left alone: it takes payment, prints, resets and reports on itself. We monitor it remotely all night and can drive the screen from our phones. For weddings over 200 guests we'll usually suggest a one-hour attended start so the first few groups set the tone, then we leave.

What happens if the printer jams or runs out?

The booth tells us before it tells your guests, and it stops selling sessions rather than taking money for a print it can't produce. Wedding Night bookings carry a loaded backup printer on site. Anything we can't fix remotely inside thirty minutes, we drive out and fix — no charge, and unprinted sessions are refunded automatically to the card used.

Does it need your wifi?

No. It has its own 4G connection and a battery-backed router. Card payments and digital delivery keep working if the venue's network falls over, and printing works even fully offline — anything queued syncs when it reconnects.

How much space and power does it need?

A 3ft by 3ft footprint for the booth, 8ft by 8ft if you want the backdrop and room to pose. One standard 13A socket. It runs on about the same power as a kettle that's never quite boiling.

Can we put our names and colours on the strips?

On Wedding Night and Residency, yes — we design the strip layout around your colours, names and date and send you a proof to approve a week before. Drop & Go picks from our existing set, which we'll happily recolour.

What about photos of children, and consent?

Guests see a plain-language notice before the first frame and have to agree to it. Nothing is used for marketing without a separate opt-in. Galleries are private links that expire after 90 days, and we delete the originals at your request, no questions.

How do we hold a date?

A 25% deposit holds it. The balance is due a week before. Cancel more than 30 days out and the deposit moves to another date within a year; inside 30 days we keep it, because we've turned other work away.
